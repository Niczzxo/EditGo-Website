Edit your videos.

EditGo is a video editor for Android, inspired by the Instagram Edits workflow. Trim, text, filters, music, speed and export — free, no account needed.

Download APK Release notes · Source on GitHub

Everything you need to edit

Home → New Project → Editor. A 9:16 preview on top, your timeline at the bottom.

✂️

Trim

Cut your clip with start and end sliders. The trim is baked into the exported file.

🔤

Text

Add text overlays on your video, with size control and live preview.

🎨

Filters

Normal, Bright, Warm, Cool and B&W looks, one tap to preview.

🎵

Music

Pick an audio file to play along with your video.

⏩

Speed

Preview at 0.5x to 2x speed, from slow motion to fast cuts.

💬

Stickers

Drop text stickers like NEW, WOW, LOVE and GO! onto your video.

🎬

Transitions

Fade, Slide and Zoom transitions between your clips.

📤

Export

Export your finished edit as an .mp4, ready to share anywhere.

Install in a minute

No account, no sign-up. Just the APK.

1
Tap Download APK
The EditGo-v0.1.0-debug.apk file (66 MB) downloads to your phone.
2
Allow installs from your browser
If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or file manager.
3
Open the APK and install
Tap the downloaded file, then Install.
4
Open EditGo and start editing
Pick a video, trim it, add your style, and export.
About v0.1.0. This is the first release and a debug build. In v1, trim is baked into the exported file; filters, text, stickers and music are preview-only for now and will be baked into exports in v2. A smaller optimized release build will follow.
